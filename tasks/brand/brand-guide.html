Logo System

The FuelBTC logomark is a hexagonal shield containing a flame/lightning bolt — symbolizing speed, power, and security. It is always used with the wordmark "FuelBTC" where "Fuel" appears in gold and "BTC" in white.

Primary — Dark BG
Light BG Variant
Gold BG Variant
✓ Do
  • Use the full logomark + wordmark together
  • Maintain minimum clear space equal to the flame height on all sides
  • Use the dark background version on dark surfaces
  • Use the mark alone only at sizes below 24px or as a favicon
✗ Don't
  • Stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
  • Change the gold color to any other hue
  • Place on a busy photographic background without a backing
  • Use "FuelBTC" as two separate words — it is one name

Brand Voice & Tone

FuelBTC speaks like a confident, knowledgeable trader who respects your intelligence. We are direct, transparent, and energizing — never condescending, never hype-driven.
